## Release notes draft

Version 3.0 makes crowded tab sessions easier to organize and share:

- Open a dedicated command palette with Command+K on macOS or Alt+K on Windows/Linux, only inside Tab Manager. The `>` alias remains available. Find actions and searchable window/group destinations, including collapsed groups.
- Right-click tabs, window headers, groups, and the full-view workspace for contextual actions. Keep selected-tab actions together in a compact corner dock.
- Drop tabs at the beginning or end of window and group headers without scrolling through a long destination. Whole groups preserve names and colors and move around other groups rather than merging into them.
- Select and copy tab titles and URLs in compact dark tooltips. Expand long text and set Title line limit and URL line limit independently.
- Copy titles, URLs, both, CSV, spreadsheet TSV, JSON, or Markdown; download CSV/TSV/JSON/Markdown. Keep the tab selection after copying or exporting, with clear feedback.
- Search one continuously scrolling Settings page with sticky headings and real previews. Choose Modern/Classic, theme, contrast, layout, and tab details in one place.
- Reset ordinary search with one Escape. Keep search rows, scroll, and caller focus when opening and cancelling the palette. Lite mode keeps quick actions close at hand and opens full view for larger tasks.
- Recover failed tab moves with Retry or Cancel. Refresh the browser state before retrying and choose another destination if the original target disappears.
- Include the preceding popup performance and memory-retention fixes from #2668.

## Store listing copy draft

Short description:

> Find, organize, copy, and export tabs across windows with contextual menus and an app-local command palette.

Detailed description:

Tab Manager v2 turns crowded browser sessions into a searchable workspace. Find tabs across windows by title or URL, organize native tab groups where your browser supports them, and act on related tabs together.

**Find and act quickly**

Search open tabs, optionally include browser history, and use a dedicated command palette. Press Command+K on macOS or Alt+K on Windows/Linux inside Tab Manager; `>` remains an alias. Right-click a tab, window header, or group to see its actions. Lite popup mode keeps finding, switching, closing, pinning, reloading, copying, and tab details close at hand.

**Organize a large session**

Select related tabs to move, group, reload, or close them together. Drop at a window or group's beginning or end, including collapsed groups. Move whole groups while preserving their names and colors, and use searchable window and group destinations. Open full Tab Manager for bulk organization and exports.

**Copy and share**

Select title and URL text in interactive tab details, expand long values, or copy them directly. Copy selected titles, URLs, both, CSV, spreadsheet TSV, JSON, or Markdown. Download CSV, TSV, JSON, or Markdown while retaining your selection for another format.

**Make the view yours**

Search a single Settings page with sticky headings and live previews. Choose Modern or Classic, System/Light/Dark theme, increased contrast, font size, window layout, and independent title and URL line limits. Use built-in shortcut help with Run buttons for applicable actions.

Free and open source. Core tab and window management runs locally in your browser. Native tab-group and Firefox container features depend on the APIs exposed by your browser. The extension does not add analytics or tracking.
